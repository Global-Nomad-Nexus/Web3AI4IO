# Data contract and comparison gate

The primary event is Pump.fun creator-fee activation at 2025-05-13 11:27:06 UTC. Public documentation appeared on May 8. May 8 through activation is an anticipation interval and cannot enter the clean pre-period.

The accepted Dune extract is `data/pump_moonshot_cohort_panel.csv`, generated by `queries/03_pump_moonshot_cohort_panel.sql`. Pump launches use exact decoded create calls. Pump migrations use raw instruction calls with discriminator `0x9beae792ec9ea21e` because the decoded migrate table matched only 30 launches. Moonshot launches and migrations use exact decoded tokenmint and migratefunds calls.

Gross cohorts use 2025-04-17 through 2025-05-07 and 2025-05-14 through 2025-06-03. Seven-day quality cohorts use 2025-04-10 through 2025-04-30 and 2025-05-14 through 2025-06-03. Thirty-day quality cohorts use 2025-03-18 through 2025-04-07 and 2025-05-14 through 2025-06-03. These earlier pre cutoffs ensure that fixed-horizon outcomes do not cross anticipation. Migration lookup continues beyond the cohort-date cutoff, so post cohorts have full follow-up.

Raydium LaunchLab is rejected as the main comparison because it began operating on 2025-04-15. Platform maturation is inseparable from the May event. Moonshot is retained as a diagnostic comparison because it exposes exact launches and lifecycle completion and predates the event. It is not accepted as a causal control because first-party repository changes occurred around the study window and users can move between direct competitors. The latter creates interference and displacement risk.

Consequently, `artifacts/h0_estimates.csv` contains descriptive relative time-series estimates with weekday indicators and Newey-West uncertainty at seven lags. These are not treatment effects. The treatment estimand is the reduced-form creator-fee rule bundle, which combines a creator subsidy, trader fee burden, and program upgrade. A staggered design remains ineligible because fewer than three comparable mandatory events across three platforms pass the registry and common-schema gates.

Chain-wide DEX volume is excluded because it does not measure launch-market thickness. No alternative control is selected after inspecting an effect sign.
