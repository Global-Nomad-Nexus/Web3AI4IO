APPENDIX B v2 -- ACTIVITY-MATCHED PLACEBO + BOOTSTRAP CIs
============================================================

Touch definition (matches main paper): >=2 cohort wallets in first-10 buyer queue.
Placebo construction: for each real cohort of size k, sample k placebo wallets from
the non-cohort wallet pool, activity-matched (launch-count within +/- 100) to each
real-cohort wallet. This controls for the wallet-activity-frequency confound that
biased the v1 uniform-random placebo.
Bootstrap: 1000 iterations, percentile method, 95% CIs.

REAL COHORT  (n_treated=5411, n_control=16233):
  Buyer-30m lift: +132.3%  95% CI [+127.0%, +137.4%]
  SOL-30m   lift: +136.5%  95% CI [+120.9%, +152.2%]

ACTIVITY-MATCHED PLACEBO  (n_placebo_cohorts=1012, n_treated=173, n_control=519):
  Buyer-30m lift: +216.3%  95% CI [+183.8%, +255.2%]
  SOL-30m   lift: +157.7%  95% CI [+117.9%, +207.7%]

VERDICT:
  REAL buyer lift BELOW placebo 95% CI lower bound.
  -> Original placebo finding confirmed under the cleaner null.
  -> Selection effect dominates; no cohort-specific causal claim.
