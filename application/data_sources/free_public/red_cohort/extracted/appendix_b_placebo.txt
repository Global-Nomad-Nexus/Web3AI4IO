APPENDIX B.1 DESIGN 1 (UNIFORM-RANDOM) PLACEBO COHORTS
======================================================
Touch definition used here: >=1 placebo wallet in first-10 buyers (loose).
Note: this Design 1 result is reported in the manuscript Section 6.4 as
context. The Design 2 (activity-matched) placebo result in
appendix_b_v2_placebo_cis.txt is the canonical preferred null.

Placebo cohorts: 1,012 (same size dist as real)
Placebo-touched mints: 21190
Control sample (3:1): 63570
Placebo treated mean (first 30m buyers): 19.94
Placebo control mean: 7.91
Placebo lift: +152.0%

For comparison: the real-cohort buyer-30m lift under the CANONICAL strict
(>=2 cohort wallets) touch definition with bootstrap CIs is +132.3% [+127.0%,
+137.4%] (see appendix_b_v2_placebo_cis.txt).
